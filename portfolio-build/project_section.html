How I work

AI as execution leverage, not a substitute for judgement.

Across these projects, I start with the problem and requirements first. AI helps me move through implementation and troubleshooting faster, but I remain responsible for whether the solution is useful, correct and testable.

Business framing: faster build-and-test cycles, quicker iteration, broader solution exploration and more independent resolution of technical blockers—without presenting generated code as the core professional value.
1 · DefineClarify the user problem, business objective, constraints and acceptance criteria.
2 · Prototype fasterUse AI-assisted development to produce and compare first-pass solution approaches quickly.
3 · Validate & debugTest behaviour, challenge edge cases, isolate failures and iterate against requirements.
4 · Own the outcomeMake the final architecture, workflow, quality and release decisions based on evidence.
